Ledgerly Pricing Plans



Ledgerly offers different subscription plans to support businesses of different sizes. Each plan provides access to invoicing tools, while higher plans offer additional features.



Free Plan



The Free Plan is designed for individuals and small businesses that are just getting started.



It includes:



\- Up to 5 invoices per month.

\- Basic invoice creation and editing.

\- Manual payment recording.

\- Access to basic customer records.

\- Standard email support.



Starter Plan



The Starter Plan is suitable for growing businesses that need more flexibility.



It includes:



\- Up to 50 invoices per month.

\- All Free Plan features.

\- Custom invoice branding.

\- Basic sales reports.

\- Priority email support.



Business Plan



The Business Plan is designed for established businesses with higher invoicing needs.



It includes:



\- Unlimited invoices.

\- All Starter Plan features.

\- Advanced financial reports.

\- Multiple staff accounts.

\- Improved business management tools.

\- Priority customer support.



Managing a Subscription



Users can view their current subscription by logging into Ledgerly and opening Account Settings, then selecting Subscription.



Users can upgrade or downgrade their plans from this section. Any change to a subscription will be displayed before the user confirms it.



Important Information



Ledgerly's subscription fees and billing arrangements are displayed on its official pricing page. Users should check that page for current prices before subscribing.



If a user has questions about a subscription or billing issue, they should contact Ledgerly customer support.
