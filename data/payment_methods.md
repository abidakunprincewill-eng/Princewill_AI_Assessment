Payment Methods Supported by Ledgerly



Ledgerly provides small businesses in Nigeria with flexible payment options for receiving payments from their customers.



Supported Payment Methods



Ledgerly currently supports the following payment methods:



1\. Bank Transfer: Customers can pay directly into the business owner's registered bank account. The business owner is responsible for confirming that the funds have arrived before marking an invoice as paid.



2\. Debit Cards: Customers can pay using supported Visa and Mastercard debit cards through an integrated payment gateway.



3\. USSD Payments: Customers can use supported bank USSD codes to complete payments where the payment gateway makes this option available.



How to Record a Payment



When a customer makes a payment, the business owner should:



1\. Log in to their Ledgerly account.

2\. Open the Invoices section.

3\. Select the invoice associated with the payment.

4\. Click the Record Payment option.

5\. Enter the amount received and select the payment method.

6\. Enter the payment date and transaction reference, if available.

7\. Save the payment record.



Payment Confirmation



Ledgerly does not automatically confirm manually recorded bank transfers. Business owners must check their bank accounts before recording these payments.



Payments processed through the integrated payment gateway may receive automatic confirmation.



Important Information



Customers should always use the correct invoice reference when making payments.



Ledgerly does not currently support cryptocurrency payments or cash collection directly through its platform.



For payment difficulties, users should contact Ledgerly customer support.
