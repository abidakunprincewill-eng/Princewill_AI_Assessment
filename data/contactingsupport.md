\# Contacting Support



You can reach Ledgerly support by email at support@ledgerly.ng, by WhatsApp at +234 800 000 0000, or from inside the app by tapping Help > Chat. Support is available Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm WAT, and Saturday 10am to 2pm WAT.



For faster help, include your shop name, invoice number if relevant, and a screenshot of the problem. Do not send your password or OTP. Support will never ask for them.



Pro users get priority support and replies within 4 hours on weekdays. Free and Starter users get replies within 24 hours on weekdays. There is no phone support on Sundays or public holidays.



If Ledgerly is down, check status.ledgerly.ng first. If the status page says All Systems Operational, try logging out and logging back in, then contact support. For billing problems, use email, not WhatsApp, so there is a written record.
