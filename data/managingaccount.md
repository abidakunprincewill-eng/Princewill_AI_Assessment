\# Managing account



To edit an account, go to Customers > Add Customer. Enter their name, phone number, and optionally email and address. Phone number is required because invoices are sent by WhatsApp or SMS.



You can group customers with tags like Regular, Wholesale, or Owes Money. Tags help you filter and send reminders. To edit a customer, tap their name and tap Edit. To delete a customer, you must first delete or void all their unpaid invoices.



Each customer page shows total spent, total owed, and past invoices. If a customer owes money, Ledgerly shows their balance in red. You can send a payment reminder from the customer page. Reminders are sent by WhatsApp or SMS. Do not send more than one reminder per day to the same customer.



You can export your customer list as a CSV file from Customers > Export. This file can be opened in Excel or Google Sheets.
