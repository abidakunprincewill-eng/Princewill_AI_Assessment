\# Account Security in Ledgerly



Protect your Ledgerly account with a strong password and two-factor authentication (2FA). Your password must be at least 8 characters and include a number. Do not share your password with staff. Each staff member should have their own login.



Turn on 2FA from Settings > Security. Ledgerly will send a code to your phone or email each time you log in from a new device. If you lose your phone, use your backup codes. Keep backup codes in a safe place, not on your phone.



Ledgerly locks your account for 30 minutes after 5 failed login attempts. If this happens, tap Forgot Password to reset it. Never share your OTP code with anyone, including someone claiming to be Ledgerly support. Ledgerly staff will never ask for your password or OTP.



Log out on shared devices. Check Settings > Devices to see where you are logged in. Remove any device you do not recognise. Report suspicious activity to support immediately.
