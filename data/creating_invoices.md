How to Create an Invoice on Ledgerly



Ledgerly is an invoicing application designed to help small businesses in Nigeria create, manage and track customer invoices.



Creating a New Invoice



To create an invoice on Ledgerly, follow these steps:



1\. Log in to your Ledgerly account using your registered email address and password.

2\. Navigate to the dashboard.

3\. Select the "Invoices" section from the main menu.

4\. Click the "Create New Invoice" button.

5\. Select an existing customer or add a new customer by entering their name, email address and phone number.

6\. Add the products or services being billed. Include the item description, quantity and unit price.

7\. Review the invoice details and confirm that the total amount is correct.

8\. Add the invoice due date and any additional notes for the customer.

9\. Click "Save Invoice" to store the invoice.

10\. Select "Send Invoice" to share it with your customer through email.



Editing an Invoice



Users can edit an unpaid invoice by opening it from the Invoices section and selecting the Edit option. After making the necessary changes, save the updated invoice.



An invoice that has already been paid cannot be edited directly. Users should contact Ledgerly support if they need assistance correcting a paid invoice.



Important Information



Every invoice receives a unique invoice reference number. Customers can use this number when making enquiries about their invoices.



Users should review invoice details carefully before sending them to customers to avoid incorrect billing amounts or payment information.
