\# Refunds in Ledgerly



A refund can only be issued for a Paid invoice. Open the invoice, tap Refund, and choose Full Refund or Partial Refund. Enter the amount and the reason. The customer must be told why the refund was made.



Refunds to bank accounts take 3 to 5 working days. Refunds to cards take 5 to 10 working days. Cash refunds are recorded manually and should be given immediately in the shop with a signed receipt.



A refund request must be made within 14 days of payment. After 14 days, you cannot refund from Ledgerly. You must handle it outside Ledgerly and record it as a manual adjustment.



If an item was damaged or wrong, ask the customer for a photo before you refund. Keep the photo with the invoice record. Ledgerly does not refund transaction fees charged by Paystack or Flutterwave. Those fees are shown separately on the invoice and are non-refundable. Partial refunds do not change the invoice status to Unpaid.
